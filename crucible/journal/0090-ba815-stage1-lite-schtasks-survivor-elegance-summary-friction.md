  approval FORMAT taken literally: one sentence of what-changes-in-your-world, one recommendation, one
  thing-that-gets-worse — ~120 words, zero plan mechanics. The failure mode: each earlier attempt
  summarized the PLAN (waves, gates, recipes) instead of the DECISION. Lesson for every gate ask:
  describe the decision's consequences, never the plan's architecture — the architecture is on disk.

  fresh-eyes both missed: 5 per-wave single-Shark reviews = recurring ceremony with no independent

  (5) Fresh-eyes earned its seat: the python-pptx round-trip hazard (save rewrites runs on untouched
  slides → keep-verbatim has no green path) was a genuinely sharp catch that became amendment A1.
